[ WHILE YOU WAIT ]

What to do until the ambulance arrives

General guidance for common emergencies — not a substitute for professional medical care.

Heart attack

  • Have them sit down, stay calm, and rest
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Only give their own prescribed medicine, if any
  • Note when symptoms started

Stroke — think FAST

  • Face: ask them to smile — does one side droop?
  • Arms: ask them to raise both — does one drift down?
  • Speech: is it slurred or strange?
  • Time: note when it started, call right away

Road accident

  • Only move people if there's immediate danger
  • Turn on hazard lights, warn oncoming traffic
  • Share your exact location when you call for help
  • Keep the injured person still and calm

Breathing difficulty

  • Help them sit upright, not lying down
  • Loosen clothing around neck and chest
  • Help them use their own inhaler, if prescribed
  • Stay calm — anxiety can worsen breathlessness

This is general guidance only. It does not replace calling emergency services or seeing a doctor.
