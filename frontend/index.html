[ WHILE YOU WAIT ]

What to do until the ambulance arrives

General guidance for common emergencies — not a substitute for professional medical care.

Heart attack

  • Have them sit down, stay calm, and rest
  • Loosen tight clothing
  • Only give their own prescribed medicine, if any
  • Note when symptoms started

Stroke — think FAST

  • Face: ask them to smile — does one side droop?
  • Arms: ask them to raise both — does one drift down?
  • Speech: is it slurred or strange?
  • Time: note when it started, call right away

Road accident

  • Only move people if there's immediate danger
  • Turn on hazard lights, warn oncoming traffic
  • Share your exact location when you call for help
  • Keep the injured person still and calm

Breathing difficulty

  • Help them sit upright, not lying down
  • Loosen clothing around neck and chest
  • Help them use their own inhaler, if prescribed
  • Stay calm — anxiety can worsen breathlessness

This is general guidance only. It does not replace calling emergency services or seeing a doctor.

Privacy policy

How we handle information in this prototype. Update before processing real patient data.

What we collect. Booking and partner forms may include name, phone, location, and email. The assistant chat stores messages in server memory for demo logging.

Why we use it. To match ambulances and hospitals, show booking status, onboard partners, and improve the service. We do not sell personal data.

Retention. Demo data lives in memory and resets when the server restarts. A production deployment will define retention, encryption, and access controls in a database.

Sharing. In a live system, pickup and contact details would be shared only with assigned fleet operators, selected hospitals, and admins who need them for dispatch — not with the general public.

Your choices. You can track a booking with booking ID and phone, or sign in to see your booking history. Contact support to request correction or deletion of your account.
